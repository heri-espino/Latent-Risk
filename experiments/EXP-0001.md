# EXP-0001 — Country index construction and smoothed-forecast failures

**Status: PROPOSED; NOT EXECUTED.**  
**Configuration:** [configs/EXP-0001.json](configs/EXP-0001.json).

## Research hypothesis
Exceedances of MLE-calibrated smoothed-trend forecasts might rise during independently designated financial disturbances beyond changes explained by existing volatility and benchmark models.

## Experimental design
Start Mexico + United States with a comparable monthly financial-indicator panel and verified publication/availability dates, then extend to UK, Japan, euro area. Compare:
- one fixed, economically signed and standardized component average;
- one-component PCA with training-only stress anchor;
- Gaussian dynamic factor model (new chronological implementation, not old source copied uncritically).

For every index, apply the same constrained second-difference Gaussian trend whose variance ratio is estimated by marginal likelihood; this algorithm remains to be implemented. Evaluate at 1-, 3- and 6-month horizons with matched origins, simple baseline forecasts and quiet controls. The full-sample retrospective factor is an additional *descriptive* track and shall not be called pseudo-real-time.

## Revised indicator-domain proposal (2026-10-17)
The user clarified M1 and M2 were examples. The initial core measurement panel should instead begin with country market equity shocks/realized volatility/drawdowns, FX volatility where economically meaningful, plus credit and funding spreads once source history is verified. Do not require money-stock levels in the primary factor. They remain separate longer-horizon explanatory candidates. Candidate external references are Banxico IEMF, ECB CISS for USA/GBR/EA, FRED STLFSI4 and NFCI, with no uniform verified Japan reference. CISS/IEMF may overlap with factor inputs: descriptive concordance is not independent predictive validation. See `data/indicator_priorities.md` and proposed ADR-0005.

## Dataset and preprocessing
Use long-form country/observation_date/available_date/variable/value/source. Audit source, units, transformations, actual availability and revisions. Use returns/changes and relevant spreads rather than automatically combining trending price levels with volatility levels. Assign country factor sign and scale from training data only. Fix identical comparable core predictors before comparative tests. Never forward-fill future releases.

## Metrics and statistical uncertainty
RMSE/MAE **within each factor target**, calibration of intervals and standardized exceedances; for cross-index ranking predeclare a **common external observable target**, e.g., future realized volatility or a documented credit spread. Quantify uncertainty over independent historical episodes and cluster/dependence robustly.

## Forecast model comparisons
Random walk, fixed-smoothness polynomial, autoregression, and factor-model transition forecast. Include volatility-aware controls and conditional stress/quiet analysis.

## Seeds and hyperparameters
No seed fixed yet; all future stochastic fits shall record seeds and convergence. MLE optimizers must be specified before running. Do not use final crisis periods to tune model choices.

None obtained. Known *legacy* failure modes: factor data leakage under ex-post measurement, selection inconsistency, Gaussian residual diagnostics. They are not outputs of EXP-0001.
