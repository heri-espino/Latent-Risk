# Mathematical research notes

This folder is a portable Obsidian-compatible vault. Use relative Markdown links and LaTeX mathematics. Put established notation and definitions in [definitions.md](definitions.md), hypotheses in [conjectures.md](conjectures.md), proved/verified results in `results/`, and source-based reading notes in [literature](literature/README.md).

Stable labels: `DEF-####`, `LEM-####`, `PROP-####`, `THM-####`, `CONJ-####`. Every substantial result records its motivating question, assumptions, exact statement, proof or derivation, mechanism, failure cases, related results, literature and verification status.
