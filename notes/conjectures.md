# Open research hypotheses

## CONJ-0001 — Regime-dependent forecast reliability

**Question:** Are historically unusual standardized trend-forecast errors more frequent near predeclared financial disturbances?

**Hypothesis (not theorem):** After controlling for country-specific volatility and ordinary benchmark forecast errors, the exceedance frequency of a specified latent-risk trend forecast may increase in designated crisis windows.

**Assumptions to test:** Stable measurement convention, proper release-date information sets, calibrated errors, event lists independent of the evaluated model, and adequate quiet-control periods.

**Potential counterexamples:** Volatility shifts alone produce larger unstandardized errors; a robust model suppresses crisis spikes; a misspecified factor drifts while no economic crisis occurs.

**Connection:** [DEF-0003](definitions.md).  
**Status:** `CONJECTURED`, entirely untested on an international panel.  
**Literature:** See `literature/references/KEY_RESULTS.md`; verification pending.  
**Next:** Freeze a common external target and crisis windows before confirming any claim.
