# ADR-0007 — Retrospective end-of-day market-feature pilot

**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** Proposed (not a finalized empirical factor or forecasting protocol)

## Decision
Prepare five country-specific equity feature sequences from official saved audit-passing market observations, with optional FX features for Mexico, UK, Japan and the euro area. Use one-day log equity returns, 21-return rolling sample volatility, and 63-observation drawdown. Sign FX returns so positive means local-currency depreciation against USD. Exact-date joins only; no forward-filling absent trading sessions or fictitious US USD/USD currency pair. Preserve CISS, STLFSI4 and NFCI strictly as separate external references; never use them as input to a factor evaluated against themselves. Exclude continuous oil futures pending roll and signed-difference audit.

## Boundary conditions
This is a descriptive, not point-in-time dataset. Trade-date labels do not prove international closing information was contemporaneously available, especially across time zones. All features at date `t` require knowledge of that date's close. No full-sample factor estimation, cross-country scaling, smoothing fit or early-warning metrics. Raw sources and derivative products are retained under ignored local paths, each with source hashes.

## Alternatives rejected
- Fill FX and country calendar gaps with same-date or next-date values: risks lookahead / fake sessions.
- Treat missing US FX value as 0: constructs an artificial measurement variable.
- Use reported ECB/FRED stress indices as both factor inputs and validation outcomes: circularity.
- Automatically logarithm WTI `CL=F` through its historic negative settlement: mathematically invalid.

## Next gate
Run the builder on VM, audit matched-date sample coverage and feature distributions. Only then freeze real-time forecast origins and factor identity, while sourcing credit and banking data for wider systemic-risk claims.
