# ADR-0006 — Instrument-aware price domains for raw-market integrity

**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** Accepted

## Question
Should all market-close price series be forced strictly positive, when some physically deliverable futures legitimately trade at nonpositive prices?

## Decision
Default to `strictly_positive` for equity indices, ETFs, FX, safe-assets and other market series, but explicitly allow `price_domain="signed"` for the catalogued WTI front-month futures `CL=F`. Keep old download manifests immutable and apply a narrow ID+role fallback only when legacy metadata omit the new field. Nonpositive observations remain preserved but produce date-stamped inspection warnings.

## Why
The CFTC and EIA document the May 2020 WTI futures settlement of −$37.63/b on 2020-04-20. Thus positivity cannot be a generic futures invariant. The user's `CL=F` series failed the old rule, but the actual offending values/dates have yet to be inspected. No claim of vendor/source correctness follows from this decision.

## Consequences
Finite numeric values, hashes, row counts, sorted dates and nonfutures positivity remain hard checks. Price-level log transforms and ordinary log returns require strictly positive inputs or ratios; negative WTI prices instead motivate an explicitly validated signed difference or separately proposed transform. Continuous-contract rolling conventions remain unaudited.
