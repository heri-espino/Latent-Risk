# ADR-0002: Separate index measurement, smoothing and failure evaluation

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** Accepted

## Problem
A large historical trend forecast error may mean a change in latent measurement, a smoothing/extrapolation failure, a heavy-tailed shock, a volatility regime shift or a structural break. An ex-post factor may have been constructed using observations not yet available at the claimed forecast origin.

## Alternatives
1. Fit a full-sample DFM, smooth once, interpret extreme residuals as early warnings.
2. Specify comparable index candidates, then a separate likelihood-based smoother, then evaluate both retrospective and chronological forecasts.

## Decision
Option 2. Begin with economically signed equal-weight composite, PCA and Gaussian DFM. Define factor direction using training data. Primary smoothing uses a constrained second-difference Gaussian trend with marginal likelihood (pending implementation). Evaluate pseudo-real-time information sets as primary evidence and keep full-sample reconstruction descriptive only.

## Rationale
This avoids circularity, unfair factor-RMSE comparisons, and hindsight-based crisis detection claims.

## Consequences
Requires an auditable release-date dataset and stable vintage target conventions. Advanced robust / switching models are conditional extensions, not completed comparisons.
