# ADR-0005 — Proposed market-stress-first measurement design

**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** Proposed (not finalized until real source coverage and a pilot protocol are audited)

## Motivation
The existing downloader catalog contained M1, M2, M4, international reserves and market-price inputs because they were convenient public series. The user clarified that M1/M2 were only examples and asked to verify indicators that make stronger financial-stress measurements.

## Alternatives
- **Money-aggregate-led factor**: incomplete conceptual and time-vintage fit, since money stocks can grow structurally and are published late.
- **Market-based factor with external references**: use equity losses/volatility, credit premia, FX shocks, funding liquidity and banking-sector stress; treat published official stress indices as external diagnostic benchmarks rather than model predictors. Proposed.
- **Directly forecast published CISS or Banxico IEMF as observed targets**: worthwhile separate exercise, but changes the estimand from constructing a new latent index; do not conflate it with the core proposal.

## Proposed decision
Prioritize the core market-based feature families. Keep money aggregates as auxiliary explanatory variables in a separate vintage-correct extension. Register ECB new CISS for USA, UK and EA plus US STLFSI4 and NFCI as reference series, with catalog roles marking them **external**, not model inputs. Mexico's Banxico IEMF is an additional official external comparator.

## Consequences and controls
Different constructs and coverage mean that raw latent-factor RMSEs are not comparable across index families. An official benchmark using overlapping variables is not a fully independent target. Credit OAS history availability is restricted at FRED since April 2026, complicating 2008 replay. Historical publication-date/vintage validity remains the gating condition.
