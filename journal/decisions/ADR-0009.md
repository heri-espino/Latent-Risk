# ADR-0009 — Preserve a separate BIS broad-NEER market-feature pilot

**Date:** 2026-10-18  
**Status:** Accepted for descriptive preprocessing; not a confirmed real-time design.

## Context

The historical bilateral-USD market feature dataset has an N/A USD/USD series by design. After the 2026-10-18 VM source audit, all five trade-weighted BIS daily nominal broad currency indices have 6,987 valid observations each, 2000-01-03 to 2026-10-13, with 2,796 explicitly missing source-date rows each.

## Decision

Do **not** mutate old vendor snapshots or the existing `data/processed/market_pilot/` version. Construct `data/processed/market_pilot_bis/<timestamp>/` as a separate version using identical equity feature methods and all-five-country BIS depreciation `-Δlog(NEER)` and trailing 21-source-observation volatility. Keep exact-date country panels plus (1) equity-only five-way intersection and (2) strict five-way equity+NEER complete-case intersection. Keep ECB CISS/FRED official stress indices isolated as external references; preserve old bilateral USD FX and DXY for robustness comparisons.

## Why and limitations

The same BIS source-methodology yields a common definition of appreciation/depreciation, but basket weights vary across countries; NEER is not itself a systemic-stress measure. BIS periodic release and possible weight revisions mean observation dates alone are not proof of vintage availability, so the dataset is **retrospective**. No forward filling, interpolation, global scaling or leakage through post-origin values. No latent index or smoothing model fitted.
