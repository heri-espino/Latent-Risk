# ADR-0008 — BIS broad daily nominal effective exchange rates for five-country FX symmetry

**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** Accepted for new-source acquisition; feature replacement proposed, not implemented

## Question
Can one common statistical definition measure currency depreciation for MEX, USA, GBR, JPN and EA without inventing a fictitious USD/USD exchange rate or relying on DXY for only the US?

## Decision
Acquire five **BIS daily nominal broad-basket effective exchange rate (NEER)** series, `WS_EER`: MEX `D.N.B.MX`, USA `D.N.B.US`, GBR `D.N.B.GB`, JPN `D.N.B.JP`, EA `D.N.B.XM`. All are indices 2020=100 with appreciation-positive orientation. Preserve the already acquired bilateral USD series and DXY as separately identified robustness candidates. No retroactive modification of old raw manifests or derived features.

## Theory / interpretation
Depreciation-oriented FX movement is `-Δlog(NEER)`, and sample volatility is calculated on the one-sided history of index log changes. Economic exposure weights and partner baskets vary across countries by BIS methodology, and changing weights affect comparability. These indices describe currency strength/competitiveness, not risk by themselves.

## Important constraints
BIS daily observation dates are not public release timestamps. Daily data are periodically published and can be revised; the API returns currently available historical series and does not create `available_date`. A model using future revisions at old origins is retrospective and cannot claim true real-time prediction.

## Rejected alternatives
Use DXY for all countries (not defined), use USD/USD with zero variance, automatically impute missing bilateral observations, or silently update an earlier market-panel manifest.
