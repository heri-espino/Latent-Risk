## Scientific objective
Forecast Mexican remittances by applying a Markov-switching model to a trend with controlled smoothness rather than only the unsmoothed observations.

## Assumptions and notation
Smoothness is controlled by a filtered trend; alternative growth regimes may be captured by a multi-state switching specification. Precise transition parameters require examination of full paper.

- **Relevance:** Avoid claiming this combination as new and include a switching model as a sensitivity analysis.
