## Scientific objective
Place Whittaker–Henderson smoothing in a modern statistical framework covering intervals, likelihood, computational efficiency and extrapolation.

## Assumptions and notation
Weighted quadratic fidelity and discrete-difference roughness penalties; generalizations include non-Gaussian statistical setups. The latent-risk study is restricted initially to a scalar Gaussian trend and country indicator inputs.
