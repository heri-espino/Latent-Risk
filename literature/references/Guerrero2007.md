## Key mathematical results
### Standard penalized trend form
- **Source:** Guerrero (2007), PLS definition early in article; exact original equation number should be rechecked before journal citation.
- **Assumptions:** Equally spaced time index, chosen differencing order and penalty.
- **Statement:** Minimization of squared data discrepancies plus a weighted sum of squared dth differences defines a smoothed latent trend.
- **Mechanism:** The penalty competes with fidelity; larger penalty weights yield lower dth-difference roughness.
- **Limits:** Not itself a cross-country stress-index or a validated crisis detector.
